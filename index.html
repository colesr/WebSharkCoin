Coin.ai & Data Privacy

Off by default. When on, your Coin.ai conversations are saved in this browser's local storage only — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Note: this stores a local dataset for you to export and use for your own fine-tuning elsewhere. Continuous background training of the in-browser model itself doesn't happen — that's not something an on-device inference model can actually do.
